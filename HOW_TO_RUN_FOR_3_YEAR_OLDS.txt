Hello little buddy! 👶
Today we are going to run a super cool magic train computer called RailETA!
It tells us when the shiny fast trains (like the Shatabdi and Rajdhani!) 
will reach their toy stations!

Follow these 3 easy baby steps. No stress, just copy and paste! 🎉

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  🛠️ THINGS YOU NEED BEFORE WE START (Ask mommy/daddy or big bro/sis):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Your computer is turned on. 💻
2. You opened the "Terminal" window (the black box where you type magic words).
3. You are inside the project folder:
   cd /Users/tarang/Desktop/HACKATHON/SIH_2026


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  🚂 STEP 1: WAKE UP THE TRAIN BRAIN (The Backend Engine)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open a brand new Terminal window and copy-paste this magic spell:

   cd backend
   source venv/bin/activate
   uvicorn app.main:app --port 8000

✨ What happens:
You will see friendly text saying "Application startup complete".
The brain is now awake and listening on http://localhost:8000! 🧠


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  🎨 STEP 2: TURN ON THE SHINY TRAIN SCREEN (The Frontend Dashboard)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open a SECOND new Terminal window and copy-paste this magic spell:

   cd frontend
   npm run dev

✨ What happens:
You will see "Ready in 100ms" and a magical link: http://localhost:3000.
Open your browser (Chrome/Safari) and go to:
👉 http://localhost:3000

WOW! Look at the pretty dark dashboard, the shiny train cards, and the live
AI arrival times! 🌟


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  🎮 STEP 3 (BONUS): MAKE THE TRAINS MOVE! (The Replay Simulator)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Want to see the trains move station to station in real time?
Open a THIRD Terminal window and copy-paste this magic spell:

   ./backend/venv/bin/python scripts/replay_simulator.py --speed-multiplier 2.0

✨ What happens:
The train will send live updates like "NDLS -> GZB -> ALJN -> CNB -> LKO".
Watch the screen update live! ⏱️


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  🧠 STEP 4 (EXTRA SMART): RETRAIN THE AI BRAIN & CHECK SCORES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you want to train the AI brain or check its test scorecard:

1. To generate 5,000 new train practice runs:
   ./backend/venv/bin/python scripts/generate_training_data.py

2. To teach the AI brain (GBDT model):
   ./backend/venv/bin/python scripts/train_model.py

3. To see how much smarter the AI is than regular clocks (62% better!):
   ./backend/venv/bin/python scripts/evaluate_model.py

4. To run all 25 automated safety tests:
   cd backend && PYTHONPATH=. ./venv/bin/pytest tests/


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ⚠️ 3 IMPORTANT GOLDEN RULES TO TAKE CARE OF (Baby Safety Guide!):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 🛑 DON'T CLOSE THE TERMINAL WINDOWS!
   If you close the black box with the backend or frontend, the train stops!
   Keep them open while playing.

2. 🛑 DON'T FORGET THE PORT NUMBERS!
   - Backend Brain = Port 8000 (http://localhost:8000)
   - Frontend Dashboard = Port 3000 (http://localhost:3000)
   Make sure nothing else is using these ports.

3. 🛑 NO FAKE/HALLUCINATED DATA ALLOWED!
   Every piece of train data is tagged as REAL or SIMULATED.
   We tell the truth only — no make-believe hallucinations!
